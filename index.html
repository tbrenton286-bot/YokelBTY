ℹ️ About of the App

YokelBTY 🇵🇬

Our Story: YokelBTY was born in the village of Papua New Guinea. The Founder grew up watching mamas sell fish, coconut and kaukau at the market, and saw how hard it was for village people to reach customers in town and around the world.

So he had a dream: From Village to GLOBAL!

Colours: Black, Red, Gold and White — colours of the PNG Flag 🇵🇬

Mission: Connect PNG villages to the world.
