About The Founder

Trenton Yokel - Founder of YokelBTY

Born and raised in Papua New Guinea, Trenton saw his people using foreign apps that didn't understand PNG life, PNG markets, PNG language and PNG pride.

So he decided to build YokelBTY — an app where PNG people can talk, share stories, sell at market, send Mune, and support each other.

Mission: One PNG. One Community. One YokelBTY.
Vision: Every PNG village connected.

🇵🇬 This is just the beginning. YokelBTY V2 is only the start.
