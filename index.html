About The Founder

Brenton Yokel - Founder of YokelBTY

Born and raised in Papua New Guinea, Brenton saw his people using foreign apps that didn't understand PNG life, PNG markets, PNG language and PNG pride.

So he decided to build YokelBTY — an app where PNG people can talk, share stories, sell at market, send Money, and support each other.

Mission: One PNG. One Community. One YokelBTY.
Vision: Every PNG village connected.

🇵🇬 This is just the beginning.
